How is HOPAXI safer than app taxis?

HOPAXI uses coordinator-led dispatch, trained professional drivers, and pre-arrival pickup instructions to reduce uncertainty at arrival. You receive clear driver and meeting details before landing.

What does privacy-first service mean?

We use only the information needed to coordinate your transfer, support your trip, and issue receipts/facturas. We do not require unnecessary profile setup to confirm your booking.

How fast can I confirm a booking?

Most bookings are confirmed within 2 minutes once you share your flight number, arrival time, and destination via WhatsApp, SMS, or phone. We commit to ≤2-minute response during your active booking window (2 hours before to 1 hour after landing).

What if my flight is delayed?

Your driver adjusts to your actual landing time at no extra charge (covered by our delay-sync guarantee within 2-hour window). We track flight status in real-time and automatically re-time pickup to match your new arrival.

What is included in the fare?

Your quote includes vehicle, driver, airport tolls, parking, luggage assistance, and bilingual coordination. No hidden fees. The price you see is the price you pay.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancel up to 1 hour before scheduled pickup: full refund. Cancel within 1 hour: 50% service fee (we reserved your driver slot). After pickup begins: charged in full. Clear policy provided before confirming.

Do you provide official receipts or facturas?

Yes. Personal travelers get digital receipts. Corporate clients get official Peruvian factura (SUNAT-compliant, tax deductible) with VAT details for business expense reporting. Sent to your email post-trip.

How will I know my driver details before landing?

Before you land, you'll receive exact meeting point instructions (text + map) and driver details (name, vehicle plate, photo). No confusion. Clear identification when we meet.
